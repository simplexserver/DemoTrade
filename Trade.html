GODS TRADER demo

SIMULATION

Simulation only. No broker is connected and no money moves. Prices are generated on this page. The results show how the rules behave. They say nothing about any real market. Demo and simulated results are not real results. Options can expire worthless. No strategy is guaranteed to make money.

How to use this page
  1. Press Run. Prices move, and a bar closes every 10 ticks.
  2. Open Signals. Each bar gets a decision. Until enough outcomes have settled, the page shows WAIT and hides the probability.
  3. Press “Force test entry” to see the trade path: risk checks, approval, execution, and settlement.
  4. Press “Next buy: socket drops” before a test entry to see an unknown outcome. The page reconciles it, and it never sends the buy again.
  5. Open Backtest to compare strategies and payouts over 20,000 ticks. Results are repeatable for a given seed.

The planted edge market is built so that the evidence gate opens after a few hundred decisions. Use it to see the trade path. Lowering “Min settled” also works, and that setting is for this demo only. The real system uses 300 or more.

Market and seed take effect after Reset. Payout, minimum sample, margin, and approval take effect at once.

Force test entry skips the evidence check only. The risk gate still runs. The real system has no such switch.
